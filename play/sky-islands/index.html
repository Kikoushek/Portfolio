Sky Islands

Click to play. WASD move, Shift run, Space jump, mouse look, click to chop, mine, build and harvest, right-click to remove.

Chop trees by hand, then press E at the workbench to craft an axe, pickaxe and building hammer. Keys 1 to 4 pick a tool. With the hammer, scroll or press Q to change piece and R to rotate. Build an Energy table to craft solar and wind power, batteries, lamps, sprinklers and robots. If the mouse does not lock, drag to look.
